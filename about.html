About NextVork

Connecting People. Building Destinations.

We are creating a simpler and more connected way to navigate the construction journey - from the first idea to the final finish.

WHY

Every journey begins with a destination. A construction project is also a journey.

A customer has a destination - their dream home, building, renovation, or project. But reaching that destination requires many different people along the way: an architect, engineer, contractor, electrician, plumber, mason, painter, interior worker, and many more.

The problem is not that these people don't exist.

The problem is finding the right person at the right time.

That is why we created this platform - to make the construction journey more connected, simple, and accessible.

WHAT

We bring the people and destinations together.

Our platform connects construction professionals and skilled workers to the projects where their skills are needed.

The People

Architects, engineers, contractors, electricians, plumbers, masons, painters, interior workers, and other professionals.

The Destinations

Construction, renovation, building, and other projects where professional skills are needed.

Our platform is the train that connects them.

Customers can discover, compare, book, communicate with, and track the right service providers throughout their construction journey.

Service providers can showcase their skills, receive opportunities, manage their work, and connect with customers.

From planning to construction to finishing, we bring the entire journey together in one place.

Planning

Find architects, engineers, consultants, and other planning professionals.

Construction

Connect with contractors, workers, and construction professionals.

Finishing

Find painters, interior workers, and finishing service providers.

Our Belief

“Every destination needs a journey. Every journey needs the right people. We connect them.”

Our Promise

“For every ‘How?’, we are the answer...”
